# Starry Habits brand direction

## Approved direction

Use the premium direction as the basis of the Starry Habits identity:

- a clearly readable `SH` monogram;
- one upward path representing steady habit growth;
- exactly three four-point stars representing small wins becoming meaningful progress;
- a friendly humanist wordmark with `Starry` in navy and `Habits` in green;
- flat, high-contrast artwork that remains legible in a mobile header and at favicon size.

The generated PNG files in `assets/brand` are visual references. They must not be treated as vector masters or shipped unchanged: the production artwork should remove all residual raster shading and convert the lettering and symbol to outlined SVG paths.

## Core palette

| Role | Colour | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary / trust | Midnight navy | `#102B55` |
| Growth / action | Growth green | `#4B8422` |
| Achievement | Star gold | `#F4B820` |
| Warm background | Warm cream | `#F7F3E8` |
| Reversed foreground | White | `#FFFFFF` |

Use gold sparingly for stars, achievements, and small highlights. Green communicates progress and successful actions. Navy remains the dominant structural colour.

## Typography

Use Plus Jakarta Sans as the preferred product and brand typeface. Nunito Sans is the fallback direction if a rounder tone is desired. The final wordmark must be converted to outlines so it does not depend on an installed font.

## Required production assets

1. Primary horizontal logo: symbol plus wordmark, transparent background.
2. Compact stacked logo for onboarding and narrow layouts.
3. Symbol-only mark for small navigation placements.
4. Square app icon with a navy rounded-square field.
5. Simplified favicon tested at 16, 24, and 32 pixels.
6. Reversed logo for navy backgrounds.
7. One-colour navy logo for documents and low-cost printing.
8. Email-header PNG with generous surrounding space.
9. Outlined SVG masters and transparent PNG exports at 1x, 2x, and 3x.

## Usage rules

- Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least the height of the capital `S` cross-stroke.
- Never add extra stars or decorative star scatter.
- Never place the logo on a visually busy background without a solid field.
- Never stretch, skew, bevel, emboss, or add drop shadows.
- Do not use the full horizontal wordmark where it becomes too small; switch to the symbol-only mark.
- Preserve strong contrast and test every asset on both cream and navy backgrounds.

## Before public launch

- Rebuild the chosen artwork as clean outlined vectors.
- Test the icon at real favicon and mobile-home-screen sizes.
- Run a trademark and visual-similarity review for the name and symbol.
- Confirm the final domain and social handles before replacing production metadata.
